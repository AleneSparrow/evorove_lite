Version note — November 3, 2026: This is the preserved policy text from the previous product. This version of Evorove uses email, a four-column board, and payment-confirmed Done. SMS, calendar booking, quotes, uploads, two-factor authentication, and model fallback are not available in this version. Account sessions use secure cookies, not browser local storage. Finding people in the open web is part of the Service. Infrastructure and subprocessor details below describe the previous deployment and must be updated before production migration.

These companies may process personal information so we can provide Evorove. The list is for account holders and is incorporated into the DPA. Marketing tools we use to post on our own social accounts are not subprocessors of your end-customer conversations.
