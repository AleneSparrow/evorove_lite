What does Evorove do?

Evorove runs email sales from the first message to payment. It opens with the person's real pain, answers replies, handles objections, and sends your offer with your payment link. You follow the entire conversation on one board.

What is the difference between Starter and Pro?

Starter is $199/month and sells to your own contacts: first emails, replies, offers, and recorded payments. Pro is $499/month and adds lead search for US companies with visible pain, with up to around 20 new people per day when verified matches are available.

Can I bring my own contacts?

Yes, on both plans. Upload a CSV or add one person at a time. Each contact needs an email and a note explaining the real pain or reason to write. Duplicates and suppressed addresses are skipped, with an import report. Your contacts start in Cold.

Does Starter include lead search?

Lead search is included in Pro. Starter runs the email sale for contacts you add. You can change your plan from the board.

How does Pro find people?

It searches public sources for US companies with a visible business pain. Each found person has a reason to reach out, a quoted piece of evidence, a source link, and an email address checked against the company's website.

What happens after I subscribe?

Add your offer text, payment link, postal address, sender name, and SMTP/IMAP mailbox details in Settings. Add contacts and their reasons to write. On Pro, add a search brief too. Keep outreach paused until you are ready to send.

What do the board stages mean?

Cold means the person has not been written to yet. In progress means the email conversation is underway. Offer made means the offer has been sent. Done means a payment has been recorded. Open a person to read the full thread.

Can Evorove change my price or make promises?

No. The engine uses your offer and payment link. It cannot invent a price, discount, guarantee, or business fact you did not provide.

Where does the payment go?

The customer pays through your payment link. Evorove records a confirmed payment on the board; it does not collect your customer's money as merchant of record. You can also confirm a received payment on an Offer made card.

Is there a free trial? Can I cancel?

There is no free trial. Starter is $199/month and Pro is $499/month. Cancel anytime through subscription management, accessible from Plans on your board.

What happens if someone asks to stop?

Unsubscribe links and stop requests suppress further outreach. Sending also respects quiet hours and your daily cap.

Do I write prompts or train a model?

No. Describe the business, keep the offer current, and give each contact a real reason for the first email. Evorove handles the sales conversation.
